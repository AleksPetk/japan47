Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 26, 2026

Plain-language summary

Japan 47 uses account and contribution data to operate the travel community. Email addresses are private, public contributions are visible to others, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Scope and operator

This Privacy Policy explains how Japan 47 (the “Website”, “Japan 47”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects information when you visit the Website, create an account, publish a place, upload an image, write a review, or otherwise use our services.

This Policy applies to information handled through Japan 47. It does not govern third-party websites or services linked from the Website.

2. Information we collect

Account information

We collect your username, email address, authentication credentials, and account creation and update dates. Passwords are handled through Django’s authentication system and are stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords. For new registrations, we also record the accepted Terms version, accepted Privacy Policy version, and acceptance time.

Profile information

You may provide a nickname and profile image. We also calculate public contributor points, badge level, and contribution statistics from your published places and existing reviews.

Contributions and uploads

We collect information you submit about places, including names, descriptions, images, city information, map and official website links, travel tips, ratings, reviews, and submission dates. Contributions are associated with your account for attribution, moderation, and ownership controls.

Technical and usage information

Our server or hosting providers may automatically process IP addresses, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, referral data, error records, and security logs. We also use technically necessary cookies or similar browser storage for login sessions, security, and request-forgery protection.

Communications and moderation records

If you communicate with us or if content is reviewed by administrators, we may retain the communication and moderation history needed to respond, enforce our Terms, prevent abuse, and document decisions.

3. How we use information

We use information as reasonably necessary to:

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure user accounts.
  • Display profiles, places, reviews, ratings, badges, and rankings.
  • Process, resize, crop, convert, store, and serve uploaded images.
  • Review submissions and approve, reject, remove, or investigate content.
  • Prevent duplicate reviews, fraud, spam, misuse, and security incidents.
  • Operate, debug, maintain, analyze, and improve the Website.
  • Respond to requests, disputes, complaints, and legal obligations.
  • Protect users, Japan 47, and third parties and enforce our Terms of Use.

If we want to use personal information for a materially different purpose, we will provide notice and obtain consent when required by applicable law.

4. Public information

Your display name, profile image or initials, badge, contributor points, published places, reviews, ratings, and related timestamps may be publicly visible. If you provide no nickname, your username is used as your public display name. Information included inside descriptions, reviews, images, travel tips, or linked pages may also become public, so do not submit private or sensitive information there.

Your email address is not displayed on your public profile. It is visible to you in your own account area and may be accessible to authorized administrators where necessary to operate and secure the Website.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Japan 47 currently uses cookies or comparable technologies that are necessary for authentication, session continuity, security, and core site functions. Blocking these technologies may prevent login or other features from working. We do not currently state that we use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking is added, this Policy and any legally required consent controls must be updated first.

6. When information may be disclosed

We may disclose information only as reasonably necessary:

  • To hosting, storage, security, email, maintenance, and other service providers acting for Japan 47 under appropriate obligations.
  • To comply with law, regulation, legal process, court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority.
  • To investigate abuse, enforce agreements, protect safety or rights, or defend legal claims.
  • In connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale, or transfer of the Website, subject to applicable legal safeguards.
  • With your direction or consent.

Japan 47 does not sell personal information.

7. International processing

Hosting or service providers may process information in countries or regions different from where you live. Where applicable law requires it, we will use appropriate safeguards and provide required information concerning overseas processing or transfers.

8. Account Deletion

Instructions for permanently deleting an account, including a summary of what is deleted and what may remain, are available on the Delete Account page.

When a normal user permanently deletes an account, Japan 47 deletes the account, password hash, email-verification and password-reset state, profile and profile image, nickname, email address, username, consent record, authentication devices, and user-specific relationships such as reviews, ratings, helpful votes, badges, favourites, saved and visited-place data, follows, collections, itineraries, and travel progress where those records exist. The former username and email address may be used for a new registration after deletion succeeds.

Places and place photographs submitted by the user may remain available. They are disconnected from the deleted account, become platform-managed, and are displayed under “Japan47 Community”. Necessary support, security, moderation, legal, or audit records may be retained where justified, but direct account links and contact details are removed or anonymized where possible. Limited information may also remain temporarily in protected backups until normal expiry.

A former user seeking a later edit or removal of retained content must create a new account and submit the request through the Contact Us form. Japan 47 will review the request and may reject it where retention remains justified by legal obligations, safety, rights, moderation integrity, or reasonable platform needs.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction. These include access controls, password hashing, upload validation, and security-related session protections. No internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your login credentials and notifying us if you suspect unauthorized account use.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and subject to lawful exceptions, you may request confirmation of use purposes, access to retained personal data, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, erasure, or suspension of third-party disclosure. You may edit certain profile information directly through your account.

We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting on a request. We may preserve information where retention is legally permitted or required, including for security, fraud prevention, disputes, or the rights of others. If a request is denied or limited, we will provide an explanation where required.

11. Children and minors

Japan 47 is not directed to children under 13. A minor may use the Website only with permission and supervision from a parent or legal guardian where required. If we learn that personal information was submitted by a child contrary to applicable requirements, we may remove the information and close the account.

12. Third-party links

Places may include links to maps, official websites, or other third-party services. Those services independently control their privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information to them. Japan 47 is not responsible for third-party privacy or security practices.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Website, law, or our data practices change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before a material change takes effect.

14. Contact and privacy requests

Requests or complaints concerning personal information may be sent through Contact Us. Include enough information to identify your account and request, but do not send your password.
